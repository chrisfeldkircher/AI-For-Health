\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

This work revisits URTIC 2017 as an evaluation audit rather than a hidden-test
score claim. In the historical experiments, a frozen WavLM-Large system with an
audit-derived prior and constrained fusion outperformed a matched
ComParE-2016$+$LR control on the same pseudo-grouped Development partitions
($0.6940 \pm 0.0157$ versus $0.5853 \pm 0.0083$). A later independent overlap
audit found, however, that the Train-centroid IDs used for those boundaries
fragment Development-local ECAPA structure. Those measurements are therefore
same-partition architecture comparisons, not speaker-disjoint estimates, and
their canonical $0.7111$ point is not an expected hidden-test result.

The ECAPA representation itself remains supported: side-local raw-space
KMeans--HDBSCAN all-recording ARI is $0.856/0.829/0.879$ on
train/devel/test; excluding HDBSCAN noise gives $0.930/0.929/0.944$ at
$97.28/96.69/97.59\,\%$ coverage. This distinction is the central lesson:
validating a representation does not validate labels transferred to a new
official side. Proxy grouping must be fitted side-locally, cold labels must
never select its representation or clustering, and whole-official-side transfer
is the load-bearing performance check when true identities are unavailable.

The historical negative-control trail---including mixup, speaker-masked
contrastive learning, adversarial heads, calibration and augmentation---remains
useful within its matched experimental boundaries. It does not constitute a
definitive speaker-deconfounding closure until retained claims are reproduced
under corrected side-local grouping or whole-side transfer.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} The exact overlap audit is implemented in
\texttt{audit\_shipped\_group\_overlap.py}; the metric-policy reconciliation and
all-recording/non-noise results are stored in
\texttt{results/speaker\_ari\_reconciliation.json}. Model and ablation artifacts
remain under \texttt{model/run.ipynb}, \texttt{cache/}, and \texttt{results/}.

\paragraph{Future work.} The immediate priority is protocol correction, not a
new backbone. Any WavLM result retained as a central paper claim should be
rerun with Development-local groups, with all choices frozen on Train and the
whole official Development side used for evaluation. The prepared blinded
pair audit can provide an identity check independent of clustering-method
agreement if requested by reviewers.
