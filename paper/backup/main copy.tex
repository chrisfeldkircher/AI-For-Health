\begin{abstract}
We present a controlled-system pipeline for the ComParE 2017 Cold sub-challenge on the URTIC corpus that combines a frozen WavLM-Large foundation model with an honesty-audited late-fusion stage. Three core contributions: (i) an honesty-prior layer-weight initialisation derived from a per-layer cold/speaker audit (A2.5, $+0.020$ UAR over uniform initialisation, $\sim$7$\sigma$ at $N{=}5$); (ii) a constrained-K admission rule for late fusion that admits handcrafted feature groups by subtractive honesty, locked at K=2 (\textsc{A2.5 + G4\_gain\_invariant + G5\_modulation}) at devel\_test UAR $0.7037 \pm 0.0060$ ($+0.066$ over the leak-corrected baseline, $\sim$17$\sigma$); a multi-K (K=1 $+$ K=2 per-seed averaging) 5-seed mean-logit ensemble reaches devel\_test UAR $0.7111$ on the canonical split, exceeding the 2017 hidden-test baseline of $0.7100$ by $+0.001$ and statistically equivalent within partition variance (a shadow-split robustness harness over 10 alternative devel\_val/devel\_test partitions gives multi-K ensemble UAR $0.6940 \pm 0.0157$, paired lift $+0.006 \pm 0.004$ over the K=2-only baseline with $9/10$ shadow splits positive; the $0.011$ canonical lift over baseline and the $0.016$ shadow-mean gap are both smaller than the shadow-distribution $\sigma$ of either single-partition estimate). A same-protocol replication of the 2017 ComParE Cold sub-challenge baseline architecture (6373-d ComParE-2016 functionals $+$ regularised cold-LR cold probe under speaker-grouped subsplit) reaches standalone shadow-mean $0.5853 \pm 0.008$, quantifying the FM-based system's lift over the classical feature-family architecture as $\Delta\,+0.109$ shadow-mean on the same strict-protocol evaluation; and (iii) a methodology framework of twelve negative-control disciplines spanning the de-confounding ladder (M8--M14: audio-splice self-control, $\alpha$-endpoint controls, bottleneck-confound, subtractive auxiliary-objective interaction, in-flight memorisation-gap, disc-ceiling-before-$\lambda$-sweep ordering, substrate noise floor), ensemble-aggregation ablations (M15--M17: monotonic-calibration invariance, small-calibration-split LR-stacking overfit, FM-substrate TTA must use perturbations that survive input normalisation), shadow-split robustness analysis (M18: cheap canonical-pipeline-logit-replay across $N$ alternative devel partitions to quantify devel-side overfit risk on small-data corpora where the held-out test set is unavailable), and a corollary about sparse minority-class corpora (M19: speaker-level aggregation increases variance rather than reducing it when minority-class speaker count is small --- on URTIC, chunk-UAR shadow $\sigma {=} 0.017$ vs speaker-UAR shadow $\sigma {=} 0.082$, a $\sim 5\times$ gap that validates ComParE's choice of chunk-level UAR as the official metric). We tested data-level (mixup), representation-level head-only (contrastive with speaker-masked positives), representation-level layer-weight-open (DANN/MDD adversary on both 128-d projection and 4096-d un-bottlenecked substrates) interventions; none beat the architecturally simpler cold-CE bottleneck. We argue the methodology framework is the load-bearing contribution: the systematic three-level closure plus the seven instrumentation disciplines are transferable beyond URTIC to any small-data paralinguistic foundation-model pipeline. We additionally tested an exhaustive K=2 candidate sweep across all six audited handcrafted groups (G5 modulation is the unique winner), a K=3 addition with the full 88-d eGeMAPSv02 superset (validates the audit-driven slicing), a K=3 addition with HuBERT-base mean-pooled as a structurally-different second foundation model (M14 pre-flight skip), and a K=3 addition with HuBERT-base \emph{equipped with} a learned layer-weighted softmax (cleared the M14 standalone threshold but failed K=3 fusion by logit-correlation with the WavLM-A2.5 anchor). The latter result quantitatively decomposed the cross-FM architectural advantage into $\sim 71\%$ from audited layer-weighted softmax pooling and $\sim 29\%$ from backbone capacity / pretraining-corpus diversity (both hypotheses partially validated; neither dominates on its own), and surfaced a refinement to the standalone-UAR-predictor heuristic --- multi-FM late fusion requires logit \emph{orthogonality}, not just individual standalone strength. Recipe-orthogonal candidates (HuBERT discrete-token histograms; HuBERT-large mean-pooled as a capacity-confirmation test) remain untested future work.
\end{abstract}
