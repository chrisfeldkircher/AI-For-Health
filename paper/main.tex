\begin{abstract}
We present a controlled-system pipeline for the ComParE 2017 Cold sub-challenge on the URTIC corpus that combines a frozen WavLM-Large foundation model with an honesty-audited late-fusion stage. Three core contributions: (i) an honesty-prior layer-weight initialisation derived from a per-layer cold/speaker audit (A2.5, $+0.020$ UAR over uniform initialisation, $\sim$7$\sigma$ at $N{=}5$); (ii) a constrained-K admission rule for late fusion that admits handcrafted feature groups by subtractive honesty, locked at K=2 (\textsc{A2.5 + G4\_gain\_invariant + G5\_modulation}) at devel\_test UAR $0.7037 \pm 0.0060$ ($+0.066$ over the leak-corrected baseline, $\sim$17$\sigma$); a 5-seed mean-logit ensemble matches the original 2017 hidden-test baseline of 0.710 within measurement noise (0.7090 vs.\ 0.7100); and (iii) a methodology framework of ten negative-control disciplines spanning the de-confounding ladder (M8 audio-splice self-control, M9 $\alpha$-endpoint controls for embedding mixup, M10 bottleneck-confound for de-confounding probes, M11 subtractive auxiliary-objective interaction, M12 in-flight memorisation-gap check, M13 disc-ceiling-before-$\lambda$-sweep ordering, M14 substrate noise floor on small-data corpora) and ensemble-aggregation ablations (M15 monotonic-calibration cannot improve UAR-at-optimal-$\tau$, M16 small-calibration-split LR-stacking is overfit-prone, M17 FM-substrate TTA must use perturbations that survive input normalisation) that emerged from a categorical closure of the de-confounding ladder plus a follow-up ensemble-aggregation sweep. We tested data-level (mixup), representation-level head-only (contrastive with speaker-masked positives), representation-level layer-weight-open (DANN/MDD adversary on both 128-d projection and 4096-d un-bottlenecked substrates) interventions; none beat the architecturally simpler cold-CE bottleneck. We argue the methodology framework is the load-bearing contribution: the systematic three-level closure plus the seven instrumentation disciplines are transferable beyond URTIC to any small-data paralinguistic foundation-model pipeline. We additionally tested an exhaustive K=2 candidate sweep across all six audited handcrafted groups (G5 modulation is the unique winner), a K=3 addition with the full 88-d eGeMAPSv02 superset (validates the audit-driven slicing), a K=3 addition with HuBERT-base mean-pooled as a structurally-different second foundation model (M14 pre-flight skip), and a K=3 addition with HuBERT-base \emph{equipped with} a learned layer-weighted softmax (cleared the M14 standalone threshold but failed K=3 fusion by logit-correlation with the WavLM-A2.5 anchor). The latter result quantitatively decomposed the cross-FM architectural advantage into $\sim 71\%$ from audited layer-weighted softmax pooling and $\sim 29\%$ from backbone capacity / pretraining-corpus diversity (both hypotheses partially validated; neither dominates on its own), and surfaced a refinement to the standalone-UAR-predictor heuristic --- multi-FM late fusion requires logit \emph{orthogonality}, not just individual standalone strength. Recipe-orthogonal candidates (HuBERT discrete-token histograms; HuBERT-large mean-pooled as a capacity-confirmation test) remain untested future work.
\end{abstract}
