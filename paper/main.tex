\begin{abstract}
We present a controlled-system pipeline for the ComParE 2017 Cold sub-challenge on the URTIC corpus that combines a frozen WavLM-Large foundation model with an honesty-audited late-fusion stage. Three core contributions: (i) an honesty-prior layer-weight initialisation derived from a per-layer cold/speaker audit (A2.5, $+0.020$ UAR over uniform initialisation, $\sim$7$\sigma$ at $N{=}5$); (ii) a constrained-K admission rule for late fusion that admits handcrafted feature groups by subtractive honesty, locked at K=2 (\textsc{A2.5 + G4\_gain\_invariant + G5\_modulation}) at devel\_test UAR $0.7037 \pm 0.0060$ ($+0.066$ over the leak-corrected baseline, $\sim$17$\sigma$); a 5-seed mean-logit ensemble matches the original 2017 hidden-test baseline of 0.710 within measurement noise (0.7090 vs.\ 0.7100); and (iii) a methodology framework of seven negative-control disciplines (audio-splice self-control, $\alpha$-endpoint controls for embedding mixup, bottleneck-confound for de-confounding probes, subtractive auxiliary-objective interaction, in-flight memorisation-gap check, disc-ceiling-before-$\lambda$-sweep ordering, substrate noise floor on small-data corpora) that emerged from a categorical closure of the de-confounding ladder. We tested data-level (mixup), representation-level head-only (contrastive with speaker-masked positives), representation-level layer-weight-open (DANN/MDD adversary on both 128-d projection and 4096-d un-bottlenecked substrates) interventions; none beat the architecturally simpler cold-CE bottleneck. We argue the methodology framework is the load-bearing contribution: the systematic three-level closure plus the seven instrumentation disciplines are transferable beyond URTIC to any small-data paralinguistic foundation-model pipeline.
\end{abstract}
