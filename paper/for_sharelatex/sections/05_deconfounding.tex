\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\subsection{Hidden-Test submissions}

For the course evaluation, five prediction files were submitted to the same
hidden official Test set. UAR is the primary metric; accuracy, precision and
F1 describe the resulting operating point. Since Test labels are unavailable
to us, these five values are single observations rather than a distribution.
They answer how the submitted pipelines behaved, not whether small differences
between them are statistically reliable.

\subsection{Corrected diagnostics}

We used two complementary post-submission checks. First, fixed G4, CQT and
equal-fusion models were evaluated by six repetitions of five-fold grouped CV
inside official Train. Split seeds varied while the architecture and zero
threshold stayed fixed. We report the mean and standard deviation over split
seeds, plus paired resampling of inferred clusters. These clusters are
speaker-correlated proxies, not ground-truth identities.

Second, we removed pseudo-grouping from model fitting and evaluation splitting
entirely. A model fitted on all official Train was evaluated on all official
Development, and the direction was reversed. For threshold transfer, six
five-fold grouped OOF runs were performed only inside the fitting side; the
median UAR-optimal threshold was then carried unchanged to the other side.
This bidirectional check measures sensitivity to the official-domain shift and
does not reuse the evaluation-side labels for selection.

We additionally reran compact and foundation-model heads in nested Train-only
outer CV. The purpose was not to reopen architecture search but to test whether
the earlier conclusion that a large frozen model was superior survived a
cleaner selection boundary.
